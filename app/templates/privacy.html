What a scan records — honestly

qrtr exists to answer “did someone scan my poster, roughly from where, on what kind of device.” It is deliberately built so it cannot answer “who exactly was it.”

Recorded per scan

  • Time (UTC), which QR code was scanned.
  • Approximate location: country and city only, looked up locally from a static IP-geolocation database. No GPS, ever — and city-level results are approximate.
  • Device class, OS, browser, parsed from the User-Agent string.
  • A daily visitor pseudonym: HMAC-SHA256(IP + User-Agent, daily salt). The salt lives only in RAM and is rotated every day, so the same person scanning today and tomorrow counts as two different pseudonyms — and even the server operator cannot reconstruct an IP from stored data.

Never recorded

  • Raw IP addresses. Used in memory for the two lookups above, then discarded. Access logs mask them.
  • Cookies / fingerprinting for tracking. The only cookie is the login cookie for dashboard users.
  • Precise GPS, names, phone numbers — nothing the scanner's phone doesn't already say out loud.

Retention & choice

  • Raw scan rows are deleted after 90 days; only per-day aggregate counts are kept.
  • Browsers sending Do-Not-Track / Global Privacy Control are counted (so totals stay honest) but get no visitor pseudonym.
  • Known bots and link-preview fetchers (WhatsApp, iMessage…) are filtered out of statistics.
